**Lab 2 – Training and Evaluating a Multilayer Neural Network**

In Lab 2, we continued our exploration of neural networks by retraining the multilayer neural network built in Lab 1, using a regularly updated student performance dataset. Every iteration generates fresh student scores across five subjects, clearly labeling each student as "Pass" or "Fail" based on their average marks.

We trained our neural network using this dynamic dataset, ensuring each iteration provided fresh insights into our model’s predictive abilities. To evaluate the model’s effectiveness, we employed an **ROC Curve** (Receiver Operating Characteristic), which visually demonstrated the network’s capability to differentiate clearly between passing and failing students. The ROC curve’s **Area Under the Curve (AUC)** provided quantitative insight into prediction accuracy.

Additionally, we introduced an enhanced interactive peer-analysis feature. This allowed us to clearly examine individual student performance in detail, comparing each student with five similar peers based on their average scores. Subject-wise comparisons were visualized, delivering practical insights into individual strengths and weaknesses compared to closely matched peers.

This lab clearly demonstrated the complete workflow—from data updating and model training to in-depth performance evaluation and peer analysis—highlighting practical applications of deep learning in student performance analysis.
